[h1]Supply Drop[/h1]

Every so often a supply helicopter flies over and parachutes a crate of good loot near one of the players. The crate's beacon draws a zombie horde to it. Grab it fast.

[h2]What happens[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Warning:[/b] "Supply helicopter inbound! Crate drops to the north-east in 45 s." A yellow arrow points at the drop zone.
[*][b]Flyover:[/b] a helicopter crosses the map with rotor sound and shadow.
[*][b]Drop:[/b] the crate comes down on a parachute with a blinking marker. One crate per player in co-op (up to 4).
[*][b]Horde:[/b] a few seconds after landing the beacon goes off and zombies converge on it. The horde grows with the day count.
[/list]

Loot uses the game's own supply-crate tables (elite box, armoury, personal and flare-gun airdrop loot).
First drop 8 minutes after loading in, then about every 20 minutes. Host can press End to call one right away.

[h2]Co-op[/h2]
The host runs the drops; crates, loot and zombies are the game's own and sync to everyone. Guests with the mod also see the helicopter, message and arrow.

Settings: BepInEx\config\girthy_mods.zedzone.supplydrop.cfg
Log: BepInEx\SupplyDrop.log

Needs BepInEx (IL2CPP).

[h2]Settings and keys: Mod Menu[/h2]
Press [b]F11[/b] in game to open [b]Mod Menu[/b] (Steam installs it with this mod). There you can rebind this mod's keys (call-a-drop key End) and change its settings without editing files.
